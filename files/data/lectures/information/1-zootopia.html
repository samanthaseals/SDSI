Data Dictionary for the Zootopia Residents Survey

About Zootopia

Zootopia is a city where every mammal (be a tiny shrew or a towering elephant) lives, works, and gets along. For the first two weeks of this course, imagine you’ve just been hired as a data analyst for Zootopia’s local government. Your job isn’t to run code for funsies, but to help city officials make data-driven decisions.

What’s in the survey

Our dataset is a survey of 30 Zootopia residents (note: this is simulated data). Here’s the general ideal of what we’re exploring:

  • Where they live. Zootopia has distinct districts: Tundratown, Sahara Square, Rainforest District, and Downtown. Residents’ reported temperatures may cluster by district.
  • Their work with the ZPD. Some residents work Patrol, others work Desk duty. City officials have made public claims about what percentage of the force is in each role.
  • Academy performance. Everyone who applied to the ZPD Academy has an exam score, and whether they passed or failed follows directly from that score.
  • Everyday life. Sleep habits and side-hustle earnings to round out the snapshot of Zootopia residents.

If you want to follow along with lecture, you can load the data with the following code,

library(tidyverse)
zootopia <- read_csv("https://raw.githubusercontent.com/samanthaseals/SDSI/refs/heads/main/files/data/lectures/1-zootopia.csv")
resident_name Categorical

The resident's name. This should not be used for analysis.

department Categorical

Which ZPD department the resident works in.

ZPD's official claim is a 60% Patrol / 40% Desk split.
district Categorical

Which Zootopia district the resident lives in: Downtown, Tundratown, Sahara Square, or Rainforest District.

academy_status Categorical

Whether the resident passed the ZPD Academy exam.

"Pass" if academy_exam_score ≥ 70. "Fail" if < 70.
academy_exam_score Continuous

ZPD Academy written exam score, 0–100 scale.

sleep_hours Continuous

Self-reported average hours of sleep per night.

hustle_earnings Continuous

Dollar earnings from a resident's side hustle.

district_temp_f Continuous

Temperature reading (°F) where the resident lives.
